          <span class="imp-eyebrow">How it started</span>
          <h2>A tiny shop with a big oven</h2>
          <p>Golden Crumb was founded by Elena Marsh, a former architect who swapped blueprints for bannetons after a summer apprenticeship in a Lyon boulangerie. She came home with a notebook full of formulas and a jar of starter she named Margot.</p>
          <p>The first shop on Mill Street had room for six customers and one very hot oven. Within a year the line stretched around the block every Saturday — and we've been baking to keep up ever since.</p>
          <p>Today a team of fourteen bakers, pastry chefs and counter crew keeps the ovens running from 3am, still using Margot in every loaf of sourdough.</p>
        </div>
