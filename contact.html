FAQ

Good to know

How far in advance should I order a custom cake?

We recommend at least 7 days for celebration cakes and 4–6 weeks for wedding cakes. Smaller orders can sometimes be accommodated with 48 hours' notice.

Do you offer gluten-free or vegan options?

All our sourdough loaves are vegan. We bake a gluten-free almond loaf and a few gluten-free treats each Friday, though our kitchen does handle wheat.

Can I pre-order bread for pickup?

Yes — place your order by 6pm and it will be ready from 7am the next morning.

Do you deliver?

We deliver catering and cake orders over $75 within a 10-mile radius. Daily bread is pickup only.
