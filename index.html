Our craft

Good bread takes time. We give it plenty.

Every loaf starts with Margot — our 12-year-old sourdough starter — and rests for up to 36 hours before it meets the oven. No shortcuts, no additives, just flour, water, salt and patience.

  • Stone-milled heritage grains from farms within 100 miles
  • Cultured butter for every laminated pastry
  • Unsold bread donated nightly to the community pantry
