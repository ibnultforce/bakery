Our story

Flour on our hands since 2014

What began as a single wood-fired oven and a borrowed sourdough starter has grown into a bakery our neighbourhood calls its own.

How it started

A tiny shop with a big oven

Golden Crumb was founded by Elena Marsh, a former architect who swapped blueprints for bannetons after a summer apprenticeship in a Lyon boulangerie. She came home with a notebook full of formulas and a jar of starter she named Margot.

The first shop on Mill Street had room for six customers and one very hot oven. Within a year the line stretched around the block every Saturday — and we've been baking to keep up ever since.

Today a team of fourteen bakers, pastry chefs and counter crew keeps the ovens running from 3am, still using Margot in every loaf of sourdough.

Milestones

Twelve years, one crumb at a time

2014

2017

Pastry case

Pastry chef Tomás joins and our croissant becomes a city favourite.

2021

We begin buying stone-milled flour directly from three local farms.

2025

Bake school

Weekend classes launch — 600+ home bakers trained so far.

What we believe

Our values

🌾

Real ingredients

Flour, water, salt, butter, time. If you can't pronounce it, it isn't in our bread.

🤝

Local first

Grain, dairy, eggs and fruit come from producers we know by name, mostly within 100 miles.

♻️

Zero waste

Day-old bread becomes croutons and bread pudding; the rest goes to the Riverside Community Pantry.
